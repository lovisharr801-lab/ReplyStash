# QuickReply — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

## What QuickReply does

QuickReply is a browser extension that lets you save and reuse text replies,
organized into categories, so you don't have to retype common messages.

## What data we collect

**None.** QuickReply does not collect, transmit, or have any access to any
data you enter into it.

## Where your data is stored

Every category and reply you create is stored using your browser's built-in
`chrome.storage.local` API. This means:

- Your data is saved only on your own device, inside your Chrome profile.
- It is never sent to QuickReply's developer, to any server, or to any
  third party, because QuickReply has no server and makes no network
  requests of any kind.
- It stays on your device until you delete it yourself, clear your browser
  data, or remove the extension.

## Permissions this extension uses

QuickReply requests exactly one browser permission:

- **storage** — required to save your categories and replies locally on
  your device, using the standard `chrome.storage.local` API.

It requests no other permissions. It cannot read the content of web pages
you visit, your browsing history, or any other browser data.

## Third parties

QuickReply does not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party scripts
or services of any kind.

## Changes to this policy

If this extension's data practices ever change (for example, if an optional
cloud-sync feature is added in the future), this policy will be updated
first, and any new data-handling behavior will be clearly described and
will remain optional.
